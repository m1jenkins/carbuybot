## 2. Competitive landscape

| Player | Model | Price | Gap vs us |
|---|---|---|---|
| ShrewdCar | Coaches you, drafts emails | $79/90 days | You still do the work |
| BuyWithAI | Free advice + drafts | Free | Advice only |
| Car Deal Coach | Scripts + doc scanning | ~$10–30 | Coaching only |
| Ranom | AI *calls* dealers | Free (beta) | Calls only, no email follow-through/close |
| Human brokers | Full concierge | $500–$2,500 flat or 1–5% | Expensive, one dealer at a time |

**Our wedge:** nobody owns end-to-end autonomous handling — search → outreach → multi-dealer parallel negotiation → closing coordination. We sit between "$79 coaching tools" and "$1,500 human brokers."

Typical broker savings benchmark to cite: $1,000–$5,000 on standard deals.

## 3. Unit economics (validate before launch)

- Target gross margin ≥ 90%: AI/API cost per engagement must stay under ~$15–25
- Support time per close: target < 30 min human touch
- Refund rate (guarantee): model conservatively at 10–15% early
- Intro cohort math: 100 × $349 ≈ $34.9K revenue + testimonial/transcript assets

## 4. Risks / open items

1. **USPTO TESS check** on "CarBuyerBots" (class 35/42) before spending on brand assets
2. **Model provider ToS:** confirm terms allow autonomous third-party email at volume
3. **CAN-SPAM:** agent identifies itself truthfully, includes opt-out in signatures; transactional inquiry framing
4. **State autobroker licensing:** spot-check CA/FL definitions — agent-for-buyer framing likely outside "autobroker" statutes, needs verification; add counsel review before scale
5. **Dealer bot detection/refusal:** personalize sends, vary cadence, cap threads per dealer
6. **Used-car complexity:** sourcing across listings + VIN/history checks; keep v1 negotiation logic shared, sourcing logic separate

## How it works

**Step 1 — Brief your agent (5 minutes)**
Tell it the make, model, budget, colors, trade-in, and timeline. Anything you'd tell a friend who owed you a favor.

**Step 2 — Your agent goes to work**
It searches dealer inventories in your area, introduces itself as your named buying agent, and runs negotiations with every seller in parallel — nights, weekends, whenever dealers respond.

**Step 3 — You pick the winner**
You get a side-by-side comparison of real out-the-door prices. Approve one, sign, done. No showroom. No pressure. No games.

## FAQ

**Do dealers know they're talking to an AI?**
Yes. Your agent introduces itself by name as your authorized buying agent and discloses that it uses AI. Dealers respect a straight shooter — even a digital one.

**Who actually buys the car?**
You do. Your agent negotiates and coordinates, you review and approve the final numbers, and you sign. We never buy cars or hold your money.

**Is this like a car broker?**
Brokers charge $500–$2,500 and often work one dealer relationship at a time. Your agent costs less and negotiates with every dealership at once. And unlike many brokers, we're paid only by you — never by dealers.

**New and used cars?**
Both. Any vehicle sold through licensed dealer inventory.

**What about my trade-in and financing?**
Your agent collects payoff quotes and lender options alongside purchase negotiations, so the whole deal is judged on true out-the-door cost — not monthly payment tricks.

**Is my information private?**
Your details are used only to run your negotiation. Never sold, never shared with dealers beyond what a purchase requires.
