FAQ

Car buying questions, answered

Everything people ask before handing their deal to an algorithm.

Do dealers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. Your agent introduces itself by name as your authorized buying agent and discloses that it uses AI. Dealers respect a straight shooter — even a digital one.

Who actually buys the car?

You do. Your agent negotiates and coordinates, you review and approve the final numbers, and you sign. We never buy cars or hold your money.

Is this like a car broker?

Brokers charge $500–$2,500 and often work one dealer relationship at a time. Your agent costs less and negotiates with every dealership at once. And unlike many brokers, we're paid only by you — never by dealers.

Do you handle new and used cars?

Both. Any vehicle sold through licensed dealer inventory.

What about my trade-in and financing?

Your agent collects payoff quotes and lender options alongside purchase negotiations, so the whole deal is judged on true out-the-door cost — not monthly payment tricks.

Is my information private?

Your details are used only to run your negotiation. Never sold, never shared with dealers beyond what a purchase requires.
