## Language patterns worth reusing

- Precise, odd-numbered anchors ($60,183) signal research, invite movement.
- Equipment/accessory verification before accepting any price — kills add-on math later.
- "No deposit for now" — polite refusal that preserves exit leverage.
- Logistics handled end-to-end (shipping, accessories, pickup).
- Courteous, zero-pressure tone; never cites competing dealers aggressively in-thread.
- Email-only discipline: creates a paper trail and avoids phone scripts.
